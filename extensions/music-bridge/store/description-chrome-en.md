Found a song on a streaming platform you don't use? Music Bridge opens it on yours.

Pick your platform once in the extension options. From then on, every track or album link from another platform takes you straight to the same music on yours: no more copying the title and searching for it.

- Works with the major streaming services, such as Deezer, Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube Music.
- Open it in the desktop app, and close the tab left behind if you like.

Privacy: the address of the page you open is sent to Music Bridge's redirect service, which finds the same music with the Odesli API. Nothing else is collected, and your settings stay in your browser.
