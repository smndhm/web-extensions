**Deezer Link is replaced by [Music Bridge](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/music-bridge/)**, from the same developer: choose Deezer and tick "Open links in the desktop app" in its options, and it opens Deezer links in the desktop app, as well as Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music links on Deezer. Deezer Link will no longer be updated.

Clicked a Deezer link and landed in your browser? Deezer Link opens it in the desktop app instead, right where you want to listen.

- Works with every link: tracks, albums, playlists, artists, podcasts, with or without a language in the address (deezer.com/fr/…, deezer.com/pt-br/…).
- Nothing to set up: install it, then open a link.
- On Firefox, the tab left behind closes on its own.
- No tracking: the extension sends no data to its developer.

Requires the desktop app, so not available on ChromeOS.
