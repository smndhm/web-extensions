No account or credentials needed. The Deezer desktop app must be installed (free, https://www.deezer.com/download) to see the redirect. Open a Deezer link in the browser, for example https://www.deezer.com/track/3135556: the extension opens it in the Deezer desktop app (deezer:// link). Without the desktop app, the browser shows its "open external application" prompt.

This version is the last one: Music Bridge (same developer, https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/music-bridge/) now does what Deezer Link does. On update (not on a new install), a background script opens once an extension page, moving.html, which explains how to move to Music Bridge, links to its listing and offers to remove Deezer Link (management.uninstallSelf, with the browser's confirmation). The content script also does nothing on a page Music Bridge already takes over (it marks the page with a data-music-bridge attribute). No new permission.
